Our Core Pillars

Select a program below to explore curriculum details, skills learned, and real-world outcomes.

Pillar 01

Civic Education & Municipal Knowledge

Understanding how local government operates is key to making real neighborhood changes. SOY breaks down complex municipal systems into actionable lessons for young leaders.

  • NYC City Council & Community Board Operations
  • Voter Registration & Policy Analysis
  • Public Testimony & Legislative Advocacy

Milestone Project

Students host a Mock Bronx Town Hall where they draft policy recommendations and pitch them to visiting community leaders and council representatives.

Pillar 02

Youth Business Incubator

Economic empowerment drives community strength. Our entrepreneurship workshops teach youth how to turn their creative passions into viable business enterprises.

  • Business Model Canvas & Financial Literacy
  • Digital Branding, Marketing & Web Tools
  • Pitch Deck Creation & Investor Presentation

Milestone Project

Graduates participate in the annual SOY Youth Business Pitch Competition, competing for micro-grant funding and mentorship.

Pillar 03

Grassroots Organizing & Advocacy

Inspired by Joel R. Rivera’s activism work, this track trains young people to identify local community issues and execute effective, positive campaigns for change.

  • Public Speaking & Strategic Communication
  • Coalition Building & Neighborhood Campaigns
  • Environmental & Social Justice Projects

Milestone Project

Students plan and lead a Bronx Neighborhood Action Initiative focused on local housing, clean streets, or youth recreation facilities.

Founder & Activist

The Vision Behind SOY

Created by Bronx activist Joel R. Rivera, Servicing Our Youth (SOY) emerged directly from community advocacy work across the borough.

Joel's core mission is ensuring young people from the Bronx don't just participate in their community's future—they actively design it through political literacy, business ownership, and community leadership.

“When we teach our youth how local policy works and show them how to start their own enterprises, we give them the power to shape the Bronx on their own terms.”
JR

Joel R. Rivera

Founder & Bronx Community Activist
